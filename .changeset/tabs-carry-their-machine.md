The window now has browser-style tabs, so you can work on a paired Mac and on this one side by side — each tab remembers its own machine and screen, with "This Mac" pinned first. Open tabs with ⌘T, the "+" button or ⌘-click on a session, and they come back where you left them after a restart. ⌘1–9 now jump between tabs, so the screen shortcuts (Agents, Devices, Runtimes, Pull requests, Notifications, Organizations) moved to ⌥⌘1–6.
