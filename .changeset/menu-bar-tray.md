Argus now lives in the macOS menu bar. A small icon styled after the app's ring-and-dot mark sits next to your other status items and pops open a dropdown of every running agent grouped by session — pick one to jump straight to that session in the main window. The icon switches between an outlined "all quiet" state and a filled "something's happening" state so you can tell at a glance whether your fan-out is still working, and the dropdown now stays in agreement with the agents screen instead of marking still-busy agents as idle between tool calls.
