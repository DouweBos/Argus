Adds secure remote access: pair a phone or a browser on your network and monitor your projects and agents from anywhere, and approve or deny agent permission prompts remotely. Argus exposes a local, end-to-end-encrypted gateway through a Cloudflare or Tailscale tunnel — or, on your own Wi-Fi, serves a web client directly with no tunnel at all. There are no inbound ports, and paired devices monitor and approve prompts by default. Set it up under Settings → Remote Access, or the Remote access button on Home.
