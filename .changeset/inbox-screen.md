The new Inbox screen collects everything Argus wants from you in one place: proposals waiting on a decision, work it has already started, and automation runs that failed. Each proposal shows how sure it is of itself and offers exactly three things to do — start it, dismiss it with a one-line reason, or open the issue it came from. You can make the Inbox the screen Argus opens on in Settings → Appearance.
